# Native permissions to inject after `npx cap add android` / `npx cap add ios`

The `android/` and `ios/` folders are generated by Capacitor and are
git-ignored. After generating them, add the entries below.

## Android — `android/app/src/main/AndroidManifest.xml`

Inside `<manifest>` (above `<application>`):

```xml
<uses-permission android:name="android.permission.CAMERA" />
<uses-permission android:name="android.permission.ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION" />
<uses-permission android:name="android.permission.ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION" />
<uses-permission android:name="android.permission.INTERNET" />
<uses-permission android:name="android.permission.VIBRATE" />
<uses-feature android:name="android.hardware.camera" android:required="false" />
```

Inside `<application>` (AdMob app id — replace with your real id):

```xml
<meta-data
  android:name="com.google.android.gms.ads.APPLICATION_ID"
  android:value="ca-app-pub-XXXXXXXXXXXXXXXX~YYYYYYYYYY" />
```

## iOS — `ios/App/App/Info.plist`

```xml
<key>NSCameraUsageDescription</key>
<string>VeriPulse uses your camera to capture verified, location-stamped photos for news reports and ownership documents.</string>
<key>NSLocationWhenInUseUsageDescription</key>
<string>VeriPulse attaches your location to reports so neighbours can verify hyperlocal news.</string>
<key>GADApplicationIdentifier</key>
<string>ca-app-pub-XXXXXXXXXXXXXXXX~YYYYYYYYYY</string>
<!-- Required by Google Mobile Ads SDK on iOS 14+ -->
<key>SKAdNetworkItems</key>
<array>
  <dict>
    <key>SKAdNetworkIdentifier</key>
    <string>cstr6suwn9.skadnetwork</string>
  </dict>
</array>
```
